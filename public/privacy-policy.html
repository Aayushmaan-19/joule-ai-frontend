Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Joule AI ("Joule", "I", "my") is an AI chat application built and run by Aayushmaan, available at joule-ai.vercel.app. This policy explains what information Joule collects, why, and the choices you have.

Information I collect

  • Account information: your email address, and your password if you sign up with email (passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; I never see them in plain text). If you use Google Sign-In, I receive the basic profile details Google shares, such as your name and email.
  • Profile information: your username, display name and avatar, including a photo if you upload one.
  • Chats: the messages you send to the AI and the AI's replies, saved to your chat history. Chats started in private mode are not saved to your history.
  • Images: prompts you use for image generation, and the generated images saved to your personal gallery.
  • People and messaging: if you use the social features, your follow requests, followers and the direct messages you exchange with other users.
  • Usage and technical data: usage counts used to enforce daily limits. For guests who are not signed in, limits are applied using IP address.
  • Voice input: if you use the microphone, speech recognition is performed by your browser. Joule does not record or store your audio.

How I use your information

  • To provide the chat, image, gallery and messaging features.
  • To create and secure your account and send verification codes.
  • To enforce usage limits and prevent abuse.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.
  • To show advertising that helps cover running costs (see below).

Service providers

Joule relies on third-party services to work. Depending on what you do, your information may be processed by:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, database and file storage).
  • Groq, which generates AI replies to the messages you send.
  • Pollinations.ai, which generates images from the prompts you submit.
  • Brevo, which delivers verification emails.
  • Vercel and Render, which host the website and the server.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the fonts used by the site.

Please do not share sensitive personal information in chats, since messages are sent to these providers to produce a response, including in private mode.

Advertising and cookies

Joule may display ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to Joule and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or learn more about opting out of third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info.

Retention and deletion

I keep your information while your account exists. You can delete individual chats and images inside the app. To have your account and associated data deleted, email me at the address below and I will process the request.

Security

I use reputable providers and reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

Joule is not intended for children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact me and I will remove it.

I may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
