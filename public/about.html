About Joule AI

An independent, cinematic AI chat app

Joule is an AI chat application designed to help you think, build and create, with an interface that feels as good as it works.

What you can do

  • Chat with an AI assistant by text or by voice.
  • Generate images from a prompt and keep them in your personal gallery.
  • Keep a searchable chat history, or start a private chat that is not saved.
  • Connect with other users through follow requests and direct messages.
  • Switch between light and dark themes.

Who builds it

Joule is designed, built and maintained solo by Aayushmaan, an independent developer. It started as a passion project and keeps growing one feature at a time.

Keeping it free

Running AI services costs money. To keep Joule available, it may show ads and may offer optional paid features in the future. How your data is handled is explained in the Privacy Policy.
